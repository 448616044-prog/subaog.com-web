About Subao Global Logistics

We're a cross-border logistics specialist focused on one thing: making it simple, safe, and affordable to ship personal belongings and household goods from anywhere in the world to China.

Who we are

Subao Global Logistics (速豹国际物流) has been moving shipments across borders for over 12 years. Headquartered in Shenzhen with logistics nodes across origin regions worldwide, we now serve seven regions: the USA, Japan, Korea, Europe, Australia, Canada, and Southeast Asia.

Today we help overseas Chinese students, expats, returnees, and small businesses move luggage, household goods, supplements, cosmetics, electronics, and personal effects back to China. Every shipment is handled by our own consolidation warehouse teams and customs brokers — we don't outsource the parts that matter.

What we do differently

  • One transparent price. Tax-inclusive customs clearance is built in. No surprise duty bills on delivery day.
  • Door-to-door by default. We pick up (in major metro areas) or you drop off. We deliver to any address in mainland China.
  • Real humans on 在线客服. 30-minute response time during business hours. No chatbots, no ticket queues.
  • Specialized handling. Supplements, cosmetics, electronics, fragile items, household goods — different categories get different handling protocols.
  • Insurance on every shipment. Full door-to-door coverage included; expanded coverage available.

Service commitment

Every shipment we accept is covered by our three-point service guarantee:

  • Quote-honored pricing. The price we quote is the price you pay. No fuel surcharges, no port fees, no hidden customs extras.
  • Transit-time accountability. If we miss our quoted window by more than 3 working days for reasons within our control, we refund 20% of the shipping cost.
  • Damage & loss coverage. Every parcel is covered up to declared value. Claims processed within 7 working days of delivery.
