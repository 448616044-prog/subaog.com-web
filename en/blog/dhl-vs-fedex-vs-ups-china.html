DHL vs FedEx vs UPS to China: Which Is Best?

DHL vs FedEx vs UPS shipping to China — 1lb/5lb/10lb cost table, customs rules, and when to pick express vs Chinese consolidated shipping.

Express cost comparison (2026 live rates)

Shipping 1lb from the USA to China: DHL Express ~$66.97 (5 days), FedEx Priority ~$73.18, UPS Worldwide Saver ~$67.75. At 5lb: DHL $94.12, FedEx $93.35, UPS $98.96 (source: Shippo/MyUS, May 2026). Heavier = pricier — 10lb runs $85–107 everywhere.

Speed comparison

DHL Express 2–5 days is fastest; FedEx Priority 3–6 days; UPS Worldwide Saver 3–6 days. All beat Chinese consolidated lines (7–10 days) but cost 3–5x more.

Customs & duty

Express carriers use commercial clearance — the recipient usually pays duty (13% VAT + 10% US surcharge above RMB 1,000). Chinese consolidated lines are tax-inclusive; duty is built into the rate.

When to choose express

Urgent items (contracts, samples, documents), high-value small parcels (reliable insurance), and B2B shipments that need invoices. Budget is secondary.

When NOT to choose express

Luggage or moving (30kg+ runs $600+ via express vs $150–200 via consolidated), daily daigou volume (save 40–60%), and sensitive goods (cosmetics/supplements get inspected or returned on express lines).

Ultimate comparison

Dimension | Express | Chinese line: Cost 3–5x | $9.5–11/kg; Speed 2–6 days | 7–10 days; Customs recipient pays | tax-inclusive; Luggage/large not economical | best; Sensitive goods not supported | dedicated line.
