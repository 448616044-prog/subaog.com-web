Shipping to China

Chinese Courier vs International Express

Chinese consolidated courier ($5-8/lb tax-inclusive) vs international express (USPS/DHL/FedEx $29-97): price, customs, speed, scenario matrix, and 5 q

Core differences at a glance

Chinese consolidated: $5–8/lb or ¥70–80/kg, 7–10 days, tax-inclusive customs, consolidation, best for luggage/moving. International express (USPS/DHL/FedEx/UPS): $29–97 per shipment, 2–6 days, commercial clearance (recipient pays duty), best for documents/urgent.

Price: the gap widens with weight

1lb: USPS $29.9 vs Chinese $8 (4x). 5lb: FedEx ¥703 vs Chinese $40 (2x). 30lb luggage: FedEx $600 vs Chinese ¥1000 (4x). Chinese lines offer the best per-kg rate at 20kg+.

Customs: tax-inclusive vs recipient pays

Chinese: tax-inclusive — duty is in the freight, recipient does nothing. Express: commercial clearance — recipient pays 13% VAT + 10% US surcharge above RMB 1,000 and may need to submit documents.

Speed: 3x faster but 4x pricier

Express 2–6 days (DHL fastest); Chinese 7–10 days. Not urgent? Chinese. Truly urgent? Express.

Scenario matrix

Luggage/moving → Chinese; daigou volume → Chinese; documents/contracts → express; urgent/high-value small → express; sensitive goods (cosmetics/supplements) → Chinese dedicated line; corporate with invoice → express.

5 questions to decide

1) How urgent? Can wait >3 days → Chinese. 2) Weight? >5kg → Chinese. 3) Item type? Sensitive → Chinese line. 4) Need invoice? → Express. 5) Budget? Save 40–60% → Chinese. Three or more 'Chinese' answers and the choice is clear.
