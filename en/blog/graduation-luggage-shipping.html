Graduation Season Luggage Shipping Guide

Graduation-season (May-July) luggage shipping: how early to book, 30-day free storage, self vs agent clearance, budget ($5-8/lb). A must-read for stud

Why you must plan ahead

May–July is the North American graduation peak: warehouses fill up, capacity tightens, and rates can rise 10–20%. Book a consultation 2 weeks ahead and ship luggage to the warehouse 1–2 weeks early (use free storage to ship off-peak).

How early to ship

Count back from your return date: air 7–10 days + customs buffer 3–5 days ≈ ship 2–3 weeks ahead. With 30-day free storage, send it early and have the warehouse dispatch when you're ready.

Packing essentials

Ship from 30kg (smaller isn't economical); vacuum-compress clothes (saves volumetric weight); bubble-wrap fragile items in sturdy boxes; seal liquids; split heavy book boxes. Good packing saves 20–40%.

Clearance: self vs agent

Self-clearance: bring passport + baggage declaration form on your return trip, enjoy a ¥5,000 luggage duty-free allowance. Agent clearance: we handle everything — for those already back or who don't want the hassle.

Budget

Chinese lines $5–8/lb or ¥70–80/kg (21kg+): 30kg luggage ≈ ¥1000–200 all-in. FedEx for the same weight is $600+.

Graduation perks

30-day free storage, free consolidation/repacking, free nationwide pickup. Group or returning customers get extra discounts — book early to lock the rate.
