How Much Does It Cost to Ship from the USA to China?

US to China shipping cost breakdown: air/sea/express rates, volumetric weight, 5 money-saving tips, and surcharges explained.

2026 rate card (luggage line)

Air (tax-inclusive): 20kg+ ¥100/kg (US tier), ¥90/kg (Europe tier), ¥80/kg (Asia tier); 100kg+ drops per tier. Sea freight discontinued. Express (FedEx/DHL): $29–97 per parcel — 3–5x pricier.

Volumetric weight is the hidden cost

Cost = the larger of actual weight or volumetric (L×W×H cm ÷ 5000). A 60×40×50cm box = 24kg volumetric. Fill the box, vacuum-compress, and you won't pay for air.

Surcharges explained

1) ¥100/shipment declaration fee (customs fixed cost). 2) Oversize surcharge for single pieces >25kg or one side >120cm. 3) Remote pickup $30+. 4) Insurance (~3% of value, recommended for high-value).

5 money-saving tips

1) Consolidate multiple orders (save first-weight). 2) Vacuum-compress clothing. 3) Ship 20kg+ on dedicated lines (best per-kg). 4) Ship off-peak (avoid graduation/year-end). 5) Book a month ahead to lock rates.

Direct vs line vs express

USPS direct: 1lb $29.9 but 15 days; Chinese line: ¥100/kg from 5kg+; FedEx: 5lb ¥703. For 30kg luggage: FedEx $600+ vs line ¥1000–200.
