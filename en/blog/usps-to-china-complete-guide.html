USPS to China: Complete Guide

USPS shipping to China: all 4 services (First Class, Priority, Priority Express, Global Express) with rates and transit times, China customs rules, an

USPS's 4 services to China

1) First Class International: up to 4lb, ~15 days, cheapest (1lb $29.90). 2) Priority Mail International: up to 66lb, ~8 days, best value (5lb $82.93). 3) Priority Express International: 6–10 days. 4) Global Express Guaranteed: 3–5 days with money-back guarantee, priciest.

Rate reference (2026 live)

1lb First Class $29.90; 5lb Priority $82.93; compare 5lb DHL Express ¥704.12. USPS wins on small parcels but loses fast on weight — 30lb luggage via Priority runs $200+.

China customs rules

Recipient pays 13% VAT + 10% US surcharge on value above RMB 1,000 (2026). Reasonable personal-use quantities can be declared duty-free.

USPS vs Chinese consolidated

USPS 1lb $30 but 15 days; Chinese lines ¥100/kg from 5kg+ tax-inclusive. The heavier the parcel, the bigger the Chinese-line advantage: 30kg luggage $200+ via USPS vs ≈$160 all-in via Chinese line.

When to use USPS

Documents, small parcels, no rush, shipping to a company address, or when a USPS counter is most convenient.

Tracking & claims

First Class has limited tracking; Priority/Express are fully tracked. Claims are paperwork-heavy — insure high-value items separately.
