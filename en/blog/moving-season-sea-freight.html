Moving Back to China: Sea Freight Guide

Moving back to China by sea: sea vs air cost comparison, furniture/appliance packing, volumetric weight, customs rules, and money-saving tips.

Sea vs air for moving

Air 7–15 days but pricier (¥80–11/kg); sea but ~40% cheaper ($3–5/kg or ¥45/kg). Furniture and appliances go by sea; urgent small luggage by air.

Packing furniture & appliances

Disassemble cabinets/bed frames (we offer disassembly + reinforcement); mark glass/fragile items; drain liquids and unplug appliances, original boxes first; wrap sofas/mattresses in moisture film. Damage risk peaks during handling — reinforcement is key.

Volumetric weight (the moving-core cost)

Cost = the larger of actual weight or volumetric (L×W×H cm ÷ 5000). A 200×60×40cm wardrobe = 96kg volumetric (actual 30kg) → disassembling halves it. Tight packing and breaking down large pieces is the money-saver.

Customs rules

Used furniture/appliances (personal use) are OK; new electronics need 3C/brand authorization; solid wood needs fumigation certificates; tobacco/alcohol are limited. Keep purchase receipts for moving items.

The moving process

1) On-site estimate (30-min proposal) → 2) Disassembly & packing → 3) Pickup → 4) Sea transit → 5) Tax-inclusive clearance → 6) Door delivery. Fully trackable.

5 money-saving tips

1) Disassemble large items to cut volumetric weight. 2) Consolidate with no gaps. 3) Vacuum-compress textiles. 4) Ship off-peak (avoid graduation season). 5) Book a month early for discounts.
