DHL vs USPS Shipping to China: Which to Choose?

Full cost & speed comparison of DHL Express vs USPS First Class/Priority: 1/5/10 lb price table, customs rules, and when to choose each

Cost Comparison (2026 rates)

Shipping 1 lb from the US to China: DHL Express ~$66.97 (2-5 days), USPS Priority ~$66-73 (6-10 days), USPS First Class only $29.9 (15 days, under 4 lb). 5 lb: DHL ¥704.12, USPS Priority ¥703; 10 lb: DHL ~$107, USPS $107. USPS First Class is cheapest for light items; DHL is fastest for urgent ones (source: Shippo/MyUS 2026-05).

Transit Time

DHL Express 2-5 business days (fastest); USPS Priority 6-10 days; USPS First Class 12-20 days. DHL is more than twice as fast as USPS, but costs more — except First Class, which is cheap but slow.

Customs & Duty

Both use commercial clearance; over ¥1000 incurs 13% VAT + 10% US-goods tariff, paid by the receiver. Chinese DDP lines include tax. DHL clears professionally but still charges duty; USPS labels are simpler but equally commercial.

When to Choose DHL

Truly urgent (2-5 days), high-value small items needing insurance & reliable tracking, documents/contracts/samples, or business deliveries needing invoices. DHL has the strongest global clearance network.

When to Choose USPS

Light non-urgent items on a budget (First Class $29.9), residential addresses that can handle customs, low-value clothes/snacks. USPS has simple labels and wide drop-off.
