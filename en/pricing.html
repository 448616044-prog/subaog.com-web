Minimum 21 kg (actual or volumetric weight ≥21 kg required; below not accepted) — shipments under 21 kg are not accepted.

Plus 100 RMB / shipment declaration & clearance fee. All-in: pickup (where available) · air freight · customs declaration · base duty · last-mile delivery to any mainland China address · base insurance.

Volumetric weight: L×W×H (cm) ÷ 5000. We charge whichever is greater — actual or volumetric.

Over-size surcharge: single piece over 25 kg, or one side over 120 cm, or perimeter over 266 cm.

Prohibited: weapons, drugs, flammable goods, gun-shaped toys, obscene or anti-government media. Restricted: live plants (need certificate), tobacco/alcohol/tea, fresh produce, meat/dairy/eggs, branded new goods (may need power of attorney).

What's included in every quote: pickup (where available) or warehouse drop-off · air/.

What's not included: oversized item surcharges (over 1.5m length or 80kg single piece) · storage fees if shipment is held beyond 14 days · re-delivery if recipient is unavailable · return-to-origin if customs rejects shipment. We quote any of these upfront when applicable.
