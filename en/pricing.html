What's included in every quote: pickup (where available) or warehouse drop-off · air/sea freight · customs import declaration · base customs duty · quarantine inspection (where required) · last-mile delivery to any mainland China address · door-to-door insurance up to declared value.

What's not included: oversized item surcharges (over 1.5m length or 80kg single piece) · storage fees if shipment is held beyond 14 days · re-delivery if recipient is unavailable · return-to-origin if customs rejects shipment. We quote any of these upfront when applicable.
