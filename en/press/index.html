Company profile

Subao Global Logistics (速豹国际物流) is a Shenzhen-headquartered international freight forwarder running country-to-China luggage lines for 12+ years. We ship from the USA, Canada, Mexico, Australia, New Zealand, the UK, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Ireland and Europe, plus Japan, Korea, Thailand, Singapore, the Philippines, Taiwan and Malaysia — to mainland China.

Air freight only, all-inclusive RMB pricing, tax-included door-to-door delivery in 10–15 working days.
