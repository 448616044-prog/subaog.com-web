2026 US-to-China Shipping
Cost & Customs Report

A cross-carrier cost study of US-to-China logistics based on public data, with an analysis of 2026 US-China trade tariff changes.

Key Findings

1. Chinese couriers / consolidated lines are 40-60% cheaper than the big four

For parcels over 5 lbs, consolidated lines charge ¥80/kg 起 vs $12-20/lb for FedEx/UPS. The gap widens with weight.

2. A 10% retaliatory tariff on US-origin goods is in effect through Nov 2026

The October 2025 US-China trade truce (extended through November 2026) keeps a ~10% supplemental tariff on US-origin goods, stacked on top of customs duty and VAT.

3. The RMB 1,000 allowance is the tax-free threshold

Personal items under RMB 1,000 (~$140) are duty-free. Accurate declaration and split shipments are key to avoiding tax.

4. Cost and speed are inversely correlated — by 20x

From DHL 3-5 days ($67-97/1lb) to , 20x cheaper.

5. Category tax rates drive total cost

Books/electronics 15%, apparel 30%, cosmetics 50%, luxury goods 50%. Wrong category can double your total cost.

3. 2026 US-China Trade Tariff Changes

In October 2025, the US and China reached a trade truce — China suspended some retaliatory tariffs while keeping a ~10% supplemental tariff on US-origin goods, extended through November 2026. For personal parcels identified as US-origin, this means a potential triple stack: customs duty + 13% VAT + 10% supplemental tariff.
