FAQ

What items are absolutely prohibited?

Weapons, narcotics, flammable/explosive materials, fresh plants and animals, meat products, politically sensitive publications and counterfeit goods are strictly prohibited.

Can I ship supplements and cosmetics?

Yes — sealed retail packaging and personal-use quantities (5-10 units per SKU) are required. We handle China customs declaration for you.

How much duty will I pay?

Personal-use items under RMB 1,000 are duty-free. Above that, rates vary by category: clothing 20%, cosmetics 50%, electronics 15%, books 0%.
