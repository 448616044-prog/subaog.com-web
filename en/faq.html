What's the cheapest way to ship to China?

Transit & timing

Door-to-door: 10-15 working days for most routes. Breakdown: 7-10 days air transit + 1-2 days customs clearance + 1-3 days last-mile delivery. . SE Asia routes are faster at 5-8 working days.

Christmas, Chinese New Year, and Golden Week can add 3-5 days — book ahead during these periods.

Yes — express air service is available for time-sensitive shipments. Typical door-to-door: 5-7 working days (vs. standard 10-15). Express surcharge applies. Subject to availability and customs clearance times. Best for urgent documents, samples, gifts, or seasonal items.

Customs & duties

Will I have to pay customs duty?

China's personal-use duty-free allowance is RMB 1,000 (about $140 USD) per shipment. Used personal items (clothing, books, household goods you've owned) typically clear duty-free.

Above the allowance: clothing 20%, cosmetics 50%, electronics 15%, books 0%. Our tax-inclusive service covers base duties — no surprise bills.

What identity documents do I need for customs?

For the recipient in China: copy of Chinese ID (身份证) or passport. For the shipper abroad: government-issued photo ID. We file all customs paperwork on your behalf — you just send us scans via 在线客服 after booking.

What you can ship

What items cannot be shipped to China?

Absolutely prohibited: weapons, narcotics, flammable/explosive materials, fresh plants/animals, meat products, politically sensitive publications, counterfeit goods.

Restricted: luxury goods (high duty), large cash amounts, prescription medication (needs prescription and doctor's note), lithium batteries (must be declared and packaged separately). When in doubt, ask our team first.

Can I ship supplements, vitamins, and health products?

Yes — we ship supplements (vitamins, fish oil, protein powder, probiotics) regularly. Each item must be labeled with English ingredients, and total quantity must be within personal-use limits (typically 60-90 days' supply per recipient). Brand-new sealed retail packaging required. We handle all China FDA declaration.

Can I ship cosmetics and skincare?

Yes — sealed retail cosmetics and skincare products are shippable. Liquid items (toners, serums, creams) must be sealed with leak-proof packaging. Total quantity limited to personal-use amounts (typically 5-10 units per SKU per recipient). Brand-new sealed retail packaging required. We handle CFDA declaration.

Packing & pickup

How do I pack items safely for international shipping?

Use a new double-walled cardboard box (not used). Wrap each item individually in bubble wrap. Fill empty space with packing peanuts or crumpled paper so items don't shift. Tape all seams with strong packing tape. For liquids, seal in plastic bags. For fragile items, mark 'FRAGILE'. We provide packing guidance via 在线客服 — photo review before pickup available.

How is volumetric weight calculated?

Air freight volumetric weight = (length × width × height in cm) ÷ 5000. The chargeable weight is whichever is greater: actual or volumetric.

Example: a box measuring 50×50×50cm weighs 25kg volumetric (air) — even if it only weighs 5kg actual, you'll be billed for 25kg. We can help you optimize box sizes to reduce volumetric charges.
